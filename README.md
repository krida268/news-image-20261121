# The World in One Image — Saturday 21 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 PNG that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026 in five
stories. Everything is drawn in code: a pure PNG encoder, a hand-made
5×7 bitmap font, and flat vector-style scenes. No photos, no network,
no wall clock — every run produces byte-identical output.

## The five stories

1. **M6.8 quake shakes Peru (lead story)** — A magnitude 6.8 quake hit
   central Peru (Junín, Andes) at 01:09 UTC on Saturday 21 November,
   felt as far as Lima, about 320 km away. No deaths, no major damage,
   no tsunami threat; Congress was evacuated as a precaution.
2. **NATO chief in Warsaw today** — Russian missiles landed in Poland
   on 20 November; NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte arrives in Warsaw
   on Saturday 21 November. PM Donald Tusk called it a deliberate
   provocation.
3. **Record foreign cash into India** — Foreign investors poured a
   record Rs 1.22 lakh crore into Indian equities over 2–20 November,
   the biggest monthly inflow ever, ending 16 months of selling.
4. **Paris braces for cost-of-living march** — 70,000 police deployed
   across France ahead of the march on Saturday 21 November at 14:00,
   from Place de la République to Place de la Nation, part of a
   three-day wave of protests in 7 European countries.
5. **KL Rahul 100 completes SENA set** — 100 off 207 balls in
   Wellington on Saturday 21 November, his 13th Test ton: the first
   India opener with Test centuries in all four SENA nations
   (South Africa, England, New Zealand, Australia).

Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, The Hindu BusinessLine — 21 November 2026.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22 (standard library only). Output: `out/entry.png`
(1024×1024, deterministic).
